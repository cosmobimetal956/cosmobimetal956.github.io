🎨 Layntra - Control Your Design Workflow Locally

Download Layntra for Windows

Layntra works as a helper for your design tasks. It connects to Figma to help you inspect designs, create project plans, apply changes, and undo actions with ease. It runs on your computer, which keeps your data private and secure.

💡 What Layntra Does

Designers often struggle to keep track of changes in large projects. Layntra organizes these steps. It uses a system that tracks your design progress from start to finish. You can view your current plan, check details on specific parts of your design, and revert back if you make a mistake.

  • Inspect: Look at your design components and settings in detail.
  • Plan: Organize your tasks to stay on schedule.
  • Apply: Send your updates back to your workspace.
  • Undo: Revert changes if the result does not match your goal.

⚙️ Using the Application

After you install Layntra, open the program from your desktop. You must sign in with your Figma account to allow the application to connect to your designs. Once connected, Layntra reads your current file. Use the interface to select the tools you need.

If you want to inspect an object, click on the design element inside your Figma window. Layntra updates the sidebar to show the properties of that item. Use the plan tool to list your next steps. When you finish a task, click the apply button to save your progress.

🛡️ Privacy and Security

Layntra stores your project data on your local hard drive. This ensures that your design files and project plans remain in your possession. The application only communicates with Figma to provide the features you request. It does not send your private design files to external servers.

❓ Common Questions

Does Layntra change my original design files? No. It creates a secondary workflow layer. You control when changes become permanent.

Can I use this without a Figma account? No. You need an active Figma account to allow the software to access your design data.

How do I remove the software? You can uninstall Layntra from your Windows settings just like any other program.
